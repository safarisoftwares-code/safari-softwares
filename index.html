Our Mission

To empower businesses and individuals across the world with practical, reliable, and affordable software that solves real problems — built with technical excellence and delivered with integrity.

Our Vision

To be the trusted software partner for businesses worldwide, proving that world-class technology can come from anywhere — especially from the heart of Kenya.

Our Story

Safari Softwares began with a simple observation: businesses everywhere struggle with inefficient systems, manual processes, and software that doesn't fit their needs. We saw problems that needed solving — and we knew we could solve them.

It started with one project, then another. Each build taught us something new. We built management systems for hospitals, construction companies, and loan providers. We created educational tools for students preparing for exams. We developed AI agents that think, reason, and adapt. Project by project, we grew.

Today, Safari Softwares has built over 15 products across 6+ industries — from ERP systems to AI-powered chatbots to educational simulators. Our code is open source. Our work is transparent. And our commitment remains the same: understand the problem first, build the solution second.

We are not just developers. We are problem-solvers, builders, and partners in progress. And we are just getting started.

"We believe great software comes from truly understanding the problem before writing a single line of code."

Every project we build is version-controlled and accessible on GitHub — transparent development from day one.
