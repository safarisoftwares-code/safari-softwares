About Us

Software built by developers who care

Safari Softwares is a focused development team based in Kenya. We build practical, reliable software and AI solutions for businesses, educational institutions, religious organizations, and professionals who need tools that work.

Our portfolio spans ERP systems, hospital management platforms, loan trackers, exam prep tools, and advanced AI agents with reasoning and document processing capabilities. We write clean, documented code and transfer knowledge to your team.

"We believe great software comes from truly understanding the problem before writing a single line of code."

Every project we build is version-controlled and accessible on GitHub — transparent development from day one.
